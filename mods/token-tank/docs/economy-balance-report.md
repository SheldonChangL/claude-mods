# Token Tank economy v0.3 — balance report

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/simulate-economy.ts` (seed `token-tank-economy-v0.3`, config version 1). Do not edit by hand; change `game/economy.config.ts` and re-run.

**No runtime verification:** every number here comes from the pure engine driven by scripted players. The per-turn usage samples are ASSUMED (see below), not measured from real sessions.

## Formulas

- effectiveTokens = input + output×2 + cacheRead×0.02 + cacheWrite×1.25
- energy = (min(eff, 15,000) + max(eff − 15,000, 0)×0.1) / 2,500
- w_i = rarityWeight × levelBoost(1 + 0.15·(lv−1)) × hungerFactor(0.5–1.0) × moodFactor(0.9–1.1); production_i = energy × w_i / √Σw
- Daily soft cap per fish (raw coins, local day): common 120, rare 360, epic 960, legendary 1,920; past it ×0.2
- Pending cap 80 coins per fish (uncollected coins past it are lost); rewards (edit/test/commit) pay coins but never lifetime coins.

Each turn jitters every field ±20 %. Turns are spread evenly over 09:00–21:00 local time. Activity 8/20/40/80 turns a day; 60 simulated days (the 7- and 30-day results are the same runs read at those days: no strategy knows the horizon).

Players: every visit collects, feeds any fish under 60 hunger back over 70 (buying food in 20s), then shops:

- **A Cheapest First** — fills free slots with the cheapest unlocked species, buys slots when full, never replaces a fish; levels fish with spare coins.
- **B Most Expensive Affordable** — fills free slots with the most expensive species it can afford; when full buys a slot, else replaces its weakest fish with a higher-rarity one.
- **C Balanced** — spends at most 60 % of the wallet per purchase on the cheapest species of the best rarity that fits; slots under the same rule; replaces a fish only for ≥ 2× its rarity weight; keeps 30 % in reserve when levelling.
- **D ROI Optimized** — ranks every option (new fish, slot + fish, replacement, fish level-up) by price ÷ estimated extra coins a day, buys the best one or saves for it unless an affordable option is within 1.5× of its payback.

Collection: every turn, once a day at 22:00, or irregular (each turn 3/turns-per-day chance; a day without a visit gets a 50 % evening visit).

## Tuning log

Every step re-ran all 240 scenarios. Token and allocation formulas never changed; only the tunables did.

Known misses at the final step, not tuned away on purpose:
- First epic / legendary medians sit a few days past the target bands because once-a-day collectors are held to 80 coins per fish per day by PENDING_CAP (kept at 80 by decision) and the Balanced player spends at most 60 % of its wallet at once. Every-turn and irregular B/D players are inside or at the edge of the bands.
- A legendary bought into a tank already full of levelled epics has a marginal payback near 40 days at 40 turns/day: with production ∝ √Σw the gain of one more weight unit falls as the tank grows. Its own production repays it in about two weeks.
- Small-context workloads earn about a quarter of the Desktop energy per turn, so every milestone arrives roughly 3–4× later for them (see the milestone table).
